Our lightning talk went really well, and I felt prepared going into it. As a non-native speaker, presenting in the US for the first time was a big challenge for me, especially since all of my group members are native speakers. They were really nice and supportive about it, which made things much easier. We looked up some materials about Costco's membership agreement, and everything went pretty smoothly. I was mainly responsible for the organization mission part, and while doing the research I also got a feel for the unique wording and style of this kind of agreement. My spoken English is still a bit of a challenge for me, but I came away with a better understanding of what this task was really about and why we chose this topic.

In the group writing task, I feel I've improved a little in my word choice for technical writing, but I'm still not very skilled at it. For example, when we worked on the paper airplane instructions, I found that my vocabulary still was not user-oriented enough. Overall, I feel the group writing task gave me a new understanding of technical writing and helped me improve in a short time.
