In class, Joe played a few YouTube videos that made one point stick with me: even small, seemingly trivial experiences can be polished to show how well you fit a position, including things like your personality and the underlying logic of how you approach and solve problems. With that in mind, I consciously tried to use numbers to quantify and polish my past experiences in my resume redesign, so that each bullet point carries concrete evidence instead of vague descriptions.

By the way, I had quite a lot of personal matters going on this week, so the resume I submitted before the deadline is honestly not the version I am satisfied with yet, and I still want to keep revising it.
